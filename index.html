5 · How it works

TapeOut turns digital logic into on-chain assets: NAND gates (logic) and LATCHes (state) are ERC-1155 components; wiring them into a circuit and “taping out” burns the components and mints a Circuit NFT whose netlist (format TAP-02 / tapnet/1) is stored by a processor contract. A circuit is pure computation — it cannot move funds or call other contracts — and anyone can evaluate it for free with eval(circuitId, inputBytes) as an eth_call. That is what makes circuit-gated products like TapeGate auditable by construction: the rule is the circuit, and this page proves the rule behaves as claimed over its whole input space.
